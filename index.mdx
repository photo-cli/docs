photo-cli is a cross-platform command-line tool that extracts when and where your photos were taken — using EXIF metadata and GPS [reverse geocoding](/reverse-geocoding/overview) — then [archives](/commands/archive) or [copies](/commands/copy) them into a clean, organized folder hierarchy with meaningful file names. Your originals are never modified. No accounts, no subscriptions, no vendor lock-in.

## What photo-cli does

photo-cli extracts two pieces of information from each photo:

- **When** — the taken date stored in EXIF metadata by your camera or phone
- **Where** — the GPS coordinates, resolved to a human-readable address using a reverse geocode provider of your choice

It uses these to copy your photos into a new folder with structured names like `2024.03.15_14.22.01-France-Paris.jpg`, leaving your originals untouched.
